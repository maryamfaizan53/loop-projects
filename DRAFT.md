# Weekly Team Update

This week the team wrapped up the migration to the new caching layer, which brought p95 response times down by about a third, and closed out the backlog of accessibility fixes flagged in last month's audit. We shipped the redesigned onboarding flow to a 20% experiment cohort and early engagement numbers look promising. The mobile team finished integrating the new push-notification service and is now running it through QA ahead of next week's release. We also patched a data-export bug that had been causing partial CSV downloads for a handful of enterprise customers. On the people side, we welcomed a new designer to the growth team and they're already ramping up on the onboarding project. Looking ahead, next week's priorities are finalizing the pricing page redesign for stakeholder sign-off, starting the database read-replica rollout, and preparing the quarterly metrics review deck.
